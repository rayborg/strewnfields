# Glorieta Mountain Meteorite Strewnfield Maps and Resources

## 1. Meteoritical Bulletin Database — Official Location

Official reference location for the Glorieta Mountain meteorite. This shows a recognized coordinate, not a complete strewnfield boundary.

- [Glorieta Mountain — Meteoritical Bulletin Database](https://www.lpi.usra.edu/meteor/metbull.php?code=10935)

## 2. Historical New Mexico Meteorite-Location Map

The paper *Meteorites of Northeastern New Mexico* includes a regional map showing Glorieta Mountain in relation to Santa Fe, Las Vegas, and other meteorite localities.

- [Meteorites of Northeastern New Mexico — PDF](https://nmgs.nmt.edu/publications/guidebooks/downloads/27/27_p0293_p0299.pdf)

## 3. USGS Glorieta Topographic Map

A 1:24,000-scale topographic map useful for examining terrain, ridges, drainages, roads, and elevations around Glorieta.

- [USGS Glorieta Quadrangle Map](https://store.usgs.gov/product/775206)

## 4. New Mexico Bureau of Geology — Glorieta Quadrangle

A detailed 1:24,000-scale geological map of the Glorieta quadrangle. It does not mark meteorite finds, but it provides useful geological and terrain context.

- [Glorieta Geologic Quadrangle Map — PDF](https://geoinfo.nmt.edu/publications/maps/geologic/gm/downloads/24/GM-24_map.pdf)

## 5. Reported Strewnfield Size

A collector reference stating that the known Glorieta Mountain strewnfield covers approximately three square miles. It does not publish a complete coordinate-based outline.

- [Glorieta Mountain Meteorite Information — KD Meteorites](https://www.kdmeteorites.com/glorieta-mountain-meteorite-information.html)

## 6. Collector Information and Land-Access Context

Background information on Glorieta Mountain specimens, hunting history, terrain, and private-property considerations.

- [Glorieta Mountain Meteorites — Spirit Rock Shop](https://spiritrockshop.com/meteorites_glorieta_mountain/)

## 7. Request for a Detailed Collector Strewnfield Map

An archived Meteorite List post showing that detailed Glorieta Mountain strewnfield maps circulated privately among collectors.

- [Glorieta Mountain Strewnfield Map Request — Meteorite List Archive](https://www.mail-archive.com/meteorite-list%40meteoritecentral.com/msg83028.html)

---

**Note:** The official Meteoritical Bulletin coordinate is a generalized reference location. It should not be treated as the center of a precise three-square-mile circular strewnfield. Detailed individual-find coordinates and collector hunting maps may not be publicly available, and access to land in the area may require permission.
