# Data rights and responsible use

The MIT license applies to original site code, not to facts, bibliographic records,
third-party publications, maps, imagery, or linked material. Each source retains its own
terms. Links and short factual summaries are provided for research and criticism; no
source publication or commercial map is redistributed here.

The Meteoritical Bulletin Database does not publish clear terms for bulk or commercial
reuse. This four-record pilot cites isolated reviewed facts and does not mirror the
database or its member tables. Seek permission before bulk extraction or commercial
republication.

Coordinates are not access guidance. A location does not establish ownership, permission
to enter or collect, or legality of possession, sale, or export. Users must independently
check current land status, regulations, and consent. Strewnfields may generalize or
withhold locations involving active research, embargoes, residences, protected or
culturally sensitive places, ecological risk, or credible safety requests.

Corrections and suppression concerns may be submitted through the repository issue
tracker. Include a public source where possible; do not post private coordinates or
personal information in a public issue.
